Use case: style-transfer / identity-preserving character expansion.
Create M1, the Stargrave inheritor (星墓の継承者), in the same drawing style and presentation as the adopted M2-M6 race concepts for the RimWorld mod 継承圏.

INPUT IMAGE 1: identity reference. Use ONLY E1 in the TOP LEFT cell, the dark stone humanoid with an ochre garment. Ignore E2-E6. Preserve E1's distinctive broad flat-topped beveled head, charcoal-grey mineral skin, small pale inset eyes, massive neck and shoulders, a few large layered stone contours and a sparse pale mineral seam on one cheek/shoulder. This is a living mineral-skinned humanoid species, not a robot or a suit of armour.
INPUT IMAGE 2: adopted STYLE AND FORMAT reference M2-M6. Match its clean charcoal outline, simple flat muted fills, restrained angular shadow shapes, plain small facial features and practical clothes. It is not a source of characters to reproduce.

OUTPUT: a single isolated FULL-BODY M1 adult in a relaxed standing shallow three-quarter view, plus one small SIDE-PROFILE HEAD inset at upper right, similar to the M board. Complete boots visible with ample margins. White background. Label exactly "M1" at upper left, no other text or characters.
Body: the biggest and heaviest species of the set, visibly broader and more massive than the Hearth worker. Very thick neck, broad barrel torso, strong stocky legs, long heavy forearms and large functional hands. Upright, living colonist anatomy. Around five heads tall, adult proportions compatible with the M drawing style; not a squat chibi, realistic human anatomy study, or bulky mechanized armour.
Head identity is essential: E1's bald, broad flat crown with bevelled corners and angular jaw. No human hair, no beard, no horns, no antennae, no elaborate ears. Quiet narrow pale eyes under a simple heavy brow. Minimal low nose plane and a short subtle mouth line. Keep the original E1's calm substantial impression. Head and shoulders are CHARCOAL GREY, not skin-coloured.
Stone anatomy: contiguous living mineral skin, using only a few wide stepped surfaces on the outer shoulders and forearms. No mechanical hinges, screws, panels, crystal spikes, armour plates, rubble or many cracks. Only two or three thin muted ivory mineral seams, one across the cheek and one along an exposed shoulder/forearm; these are matte, not glowing. Large hands are simple humanoid hands, not rock-clump mittens.
Clothes: translate E1's ochre cloth into a practical plain ochre sleeveless cross-body work tunic with a broad diagonal neckline revealing part of the mineral shoulder, tied with a simple dark cloth belt. Durable dark brown/charcoal straight work trousers and plain broad boots sized to the heavy legs. Few folds, no buckles collection, straps collection, tools, weapons, sigils or ornate fantasy dress. The cloth must visibly sit over the mineral body rather than be part of its skin.
Style: precisely as simple as the second reference, broad flat colour regions and small restrained hard shadows, no gradients, material grain, paper texture, soft rendering, glossy rock, dust, specular lighting, high-detail anatomy or dramatic pose. Avoid generic anime glamour. This is a plain practical adult person who builds a colony. Sparse line choices, readable silhouette and species features.
Do not alter, reproduce, replace or include M2-M6. Only produce the M1 full-body plus small profile-head concept, harmonizing with their drawing style while retaining E1's adopted stone identity.

# M案の生成指示

使用: built-in image_gen。第1回は既存設定を言語化した新規案（画像参照なし）。第2回は初回Mを編集対象、E-sheetを線・省略の参考にした。E画像は事前に視覚確認済み、E1を再描画対象に含めない。ともに不透明背景。

## 初回：種族特徴の描き直し

```text

No grid background, environment or decorative frame. Similar ground line; adults have deliberately different heights and shoulder widths. Sparse purposeful marks. Slightly angular, utilitarian, plain people with individual character. Adult imperfect faces, varied jaw/nose/brow, some age and work-worn character but no realistic skin rendering. NO polished manga glamour, elaborate spiky hairstyle, eyelashes, baby face, airbrush, gradient, texture/noise filter, elaborate cloth folds, ornate fantasy gear or smooth identical faces. M4 WHITECROWN / furred horned humanoid. Adult man, upright human posture and clearly human nose/mouth, broad but not as heavy as M3. Wine-red short sleeveless practical coat over plain dark shirt, dark trousers and fitted boots. Geometric, minimal clothing and no royal jewellery. M5 TIDERING / saline-adapted amphibious humanoid. Adult man of normal human build, blue-grey skin, human face with rounded nose, broad cheek shape and small quiet eyes. Rust-orange plain sleeveless waist-length field vest over pale undershirt, grey practical fitted trousers, short waterproof boots, one simple water flask at the hip. ## 最終：ゲーム向けに線・質感を省略

This is simple illustrated concept art for a colony game, not a polished high-detail splash illustration.
